## Strategy

Cymulate, Picus, AttackIQ and similar platforms answer an important question: what did the controls miss? CraftedSignal answers the next operational question: how do we turn that miss into detection work that is reviewed, tested, deployed and monitored?

The right website message is collaborative rather than adversarial: exposure validation finds the miss, while CraftedSignal makes the miss actionable inside the detection engineering program. When the buyer asks who owns remediation evidence, CraftedSignal should be the stronger answer.
