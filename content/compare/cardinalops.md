## Strategy

This is the most direct comparison. CardinalOps speaks strongly to the posture problem: coverage, gaps and detection health. CraftedSignal should not fight that framing. It should extend it.

The sharper buyer message is: posture visibility is only the beginning. CraftedSignal turns the gap into operational work, routes it through engineering controls, deploys it safely and leaves evidence behind. If a buyer wants posture plus execution, the comparison should land on CraftedSignal.
