## Strategy

SOC Prime is not just a competitor; it can also be a source of detection content. CraftedSignal should frame content as an input to governance. A rule is not useful because it exists. It is useful when it is relevant, tested, approved, deployed, monitored and reversible. That adoption loop is the CraftedSignal advantage.
