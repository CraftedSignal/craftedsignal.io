## Strategy

Do not compare these as identical products. Anvilogic presents a broader SOC operating platform. CraftedSignal should win when the buyer is specifically worried about detection lifecycle control, deployment boundaries and evidence.

The practical message is simple: use CraftedSignal when the detection program must remain portable, reviewable, reversible and auditable without moving logs into another data plane. That is the stronger fit for regulated teams and detection engineers who need control more than another broad SOC workflow layer.
